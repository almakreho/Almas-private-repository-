# AIDA 1141 Week 5 — Graded Lab 2 rubric

**Total: 5% of the course grade. One lab, one folder, one submission.**

| Component | Evidence assessed | Weight |
|---|---|---:|
| Guided Part A — Classification | Correctly identifies the three features and `reorder_now` as the category label; trains the supplied classification model and generates both product predictions in the required CSV. | 1.50% |
| Guided Part A — Interpretation | Reports the two category predictions and explains that staff should review them rather than treating them as certain or automatic decisions. | 1.50% |
| Independent Part B — Regression | Independently selects the features and numeric label, trains the regression model, and generates both numeric estimates in the required CSV. | 1.00% |
| Independent Part B — Decision note | Reports estimates accurately, describes one limitation, and gives one reasonable evidence-based next step. | 1.00% |
| **Total** | **All required work is in the same Lab 2 folder and submitted once.** | **5.00%** |

Both components are required parts of this single graded lab. The guided and independent components are not separate GitHub folders or D2L submissions. Follow the README; no additional deliverable is implied by this rubric.
